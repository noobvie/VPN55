@@ routing

All traffic goes through the tunnel. IPv6 is captured and dropped rather
  than routed, so it fails closed instead of leaving in the clear.

@@ transport.awg

TRANSPORT: AmneziaWG. This needs a client that speaks it — the stock
  WireGuard app will reject the Jc/S1/H1 lines below, and deleting them
  does not fall back to WireGuard, it just stops connecting.
  Those values are this server's and must be carried exactly as they
  are; they are not secret and they are not tunable.
